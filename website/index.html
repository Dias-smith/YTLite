Purpose of Mixtube

What Mixtube is — and why it uses Google APIs

Mixtube (also referred to historically as YTLite in some technical identifiers) is a consumer media app. Its purpose is to let people discover and play online video and music, keep a personal library (playlists, likes, history), optionally sync that library when signed in, and optionally import their own audio files from sources they choose — including Files, Wi‑Fi transfer, and Google Drive (read-only).

This homepage is public. You do not need to create an account or sign in to read about Mixtube, its features, or our Privacy Policy and Terms.

  • Core product

    Browse and search online video, build queues, use a mini player and sleep timer, and manage playlists and history on device — with optional cloud sync after sign-in.

  • Optional Google Sign-In

    If you sign in with Google, Mixtube may request read-only Google user data (for example youtube.readonly) so we can show subscriptions, playlists, and liked items you already have — display only, never to modify your Google content.

  • Optional Google Drive import

    When you choose Import → Google Drive, Mixtube may request drive.readonly solely so you can browse audio/video files in your Drive and download selected files to this device for offline playback in your local Imported library. We do not upload Drive files to our servers, do not change or share Drive content, and do not use Drive data for advertising or AI model training.

Google APIs Terms compliance. Mixtube does not use Google APIs to create, generate, or distribute AI-generated non-consensual intimate imagery (AI NCII). Mixtube does not use Google user data (including YouTube or Drive data) to train or improve generalized machine-learning or AI models. Use of Google user data is limited to the in-app features described here and in our Privacy Policy.
